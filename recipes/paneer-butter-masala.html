Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer Butter Masala is a creamy tomato-based curry with soft paneer cubes. It is rich, flavorful, and great for a special lunch or dinner.

This dish is often served with naan or rice and is a favorite for many people who enjoy mild Indian curries.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 cup cream

Steps

  1. Cook the onion, garlic, ginger, and tomatoes until soft.
  2. Blend the mixture into a smooth sauce and return it to the pan.
  3. Add butter, garam masala, and cream, then stir well.
  4. Add paneer cubes and cook until heated through before serving.
