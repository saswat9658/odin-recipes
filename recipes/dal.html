Dal

Description

Dal is a simple and comforting lentil dish that is popular in many Indian homes. It is easy to make and works well with rice or flatbread.

This recipe uses basic spices and a soft, creamy texture that makes it perfect for a quick everyday meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow lentils
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the lentils thoroughly and cook them with water until soft.
  2. Saute the onion, garlic, tomato, and spices in a pan.
  3. Add the cooked lentils and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Season with salt and serve warm.
