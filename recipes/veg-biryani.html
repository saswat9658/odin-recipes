Veg Biryani

Description

Veg Biryani is a fragrant rice dish layered with vegetables and warm spices. It is colorful, satisfying, and perfect for sharing with family.

Cooking it in layers gives the rice a wonderful flavor and makes the dish feel festive and special.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon biryani masala
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the rice until it is partly done and set it aside.
  2. Saute the onion, garlic, ginger, and vegetables in oil.
  3. Layer the rice and vegetable mixture with spices and cook gently.
  4. Serve hot once the flavors have blended together.
